Recently Read & Listened

One line on what stayed with me.

The Midnight Library

Matt Haig
Read · Sep 2026

The idea of life as a quantum state: endless parallel versions, all one decision apart. What stuck with me most is that the path I didn't take wasn't necessarily the better one, so there's no point living with regret over choices I didn't make. It left me feeling warm, grateful, and a lot more present. Loved it, and glad this was the book that got me reading again.

The Psychology of Money

Morgan Housel
Listened · Sep 2026

Loved this one. I wouldn't say I learned a ton about money specifically. It's more a book about human psychology and behavior, and how that shapes a lot of life choices, money being just one of them.
