anaghabelavadi.github.io

My personal website — a mix of research portfolio and personal corner of the internet.

🔗 Live site: anaghabelavadi.github.io

What's here
Home (index.html) — intro and links to everything else
Research Portfolio (research-portfolio.html) — my work in materials science, sustainability, and dynamic material flow analysis
Research Stories (research-stories.html) — more narrative writeups of specific projects
Travel (travel.html) — places I've been
Hobbies (hobbies.html) — strength training, endurance training/running, crochet
Bookshelf (reading.html) — what I'm currently reading/listening to, and a log of what I've recently finished


Built with
Static HTML/CSS/JS — no build step, no framework. Based on the Story template by HTML5 UP, used under the CCA 3.0 license (attribution in the site footer).
header.html / footer.html are shared across pages via a small custom include system (include.js).

Editing
Pages are plain .html files — edit directly.
Images live under images/<section>/; keep them reasonably compressed (aim for well under 1 MB, resized to ~1600px on the long side) so pages load fast.
After renaming or replacing an image, add a ?v=2-style query string to its src if the old version seems to be cached somewhere.
Nav links live in two places — header.html and the inline nav inside index.html's banner — update both when adding a page.
