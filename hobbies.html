Strength Training

Strength training has become a non-negotiable. I've been lifting consistently for about three years, usually 3–4 sessions a week, and the change in my body composition, energy levels, and how much I can lift has been very visible. Building and keeping muscle is especially important for women, and knowing that has only made me more committed. Honestly, I just love being in the gym.

Endurance Training

Lately I've been very into endurance training, mainly running, and the things it has taught me are a little crazy. Discipline, for one. But the bigger lesson is that the limiting factor in most things in life is my mind — it's a mind game far more than it is a leg game. I've been signing up for races this year, and while I don't think the race itself adds much on its own, the training for it does: I have a schedule to follow, it pushes me off my phone in the evenings, and I waste far less time than I used to. Somehow, running has given me more time for everything else, which is the truth behind the old saying that if you want something done, you should ask a busy person. I've started reading more about the science of endurance too, and one of my lifetime goals is to finish an Ironman or at least a 70.3.
